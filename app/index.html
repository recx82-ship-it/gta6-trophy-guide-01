Roadmap to the platinum

Five stages, in order. PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 and puts the platinum at roughly 200 hours, in a single playthrough. There is no mission replay and cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep cheats off and keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter. Free-roam stays open after the story.
